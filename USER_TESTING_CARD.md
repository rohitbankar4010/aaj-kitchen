# aaj. — Five-Person Usability Test (do this before final HelloPM submission)

**Goal:** Can an actual Indian home cook choose a practical dinner in under 60 seconds without being coached?

**Recruit 3–5 people who sometimes cook their own meals.** Ask their permission to record time, comments, and optionally their screen. Do not count invented interviews or friends watching a demo as completing the test themselves.

## Script to read to each tester

> Open this link: https://aaj-kitchen.vercel.app/. Imagine you're deciding what to cook tonight using what is in your kitchen. Use aaj. without me explaining where to tap. Stop when you've selected a dish you would realistically cook. Tell me if anything is confusing or inaccurate.

## Additional 2-minute checks (after time-to-decision measurement)

1. Change one pantry ingredient to unavailable. Does the meal recommendation respond truthfully?
2. Show the My Masala Box. Can they understand remembered vs confirmed today vs out?
3. Open a recipe missing an essential. Can they find a substitution or shopping list?
4. Read one cooking instruction and view its illustration. Does the visual actually help?

## Suggested **targets**, not claimed results

- At least 4 of 5 users identify a realistic meal within 60 seconds, without assistance.
- Zero cases where a dish is described as **confirmed ready** while an essential is explicitly unavailable.
- At least 4 of 5 users understand where to change kitchen ingredients.
- Clear evidence of **one feedback-driven iteration**, including before/after screenshots.

For your HelloPM slide: report real counts and timings, not only a satisfaction average. If the product fails, show the actual failure and what changed. This is credible product thinking.
