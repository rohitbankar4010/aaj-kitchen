aaj. — Mobile testing update (26 November 2026)

The original app is tablet-first. This update includes responsive improvements for phones:
- Stacked header, prominent My Kitchen + Find a Meal actions, scrolling mood choices.
- Compact 3D art after the question, before the featured recipe; readable recipe actions.
- Swipeable alternative meals and compact recipe preview sheet.
- Full-height Kitchen Memory, first-open Kitchen Check, and grocery shopping list.
- Cooking instructions with a visual scene above the text and reachable Next/Previous navigation.

The existing ingredient checks, local recommendation logic, 30 recipes, shopping handoff, animations and localStorage keys are unchanged.

TEST ON REAL PHONES (not just desktop):
1. Open https://aaj-kitchen.vercel.app in a private tab on Android or iPhone.
2. On first visit, use or skip Kitchen Check.
3. Scroll mood choices, open Find a Meal, select a recipe.
4. Open My Kitchen; toggle one ingredient; ensure list and count update without losing scroll.
5. Start cooking, verify the step illustration, ingredient recovery and Next/Previous.
6. Open Get Missing Ingredients, edit/copy list and use Instamart handoff.
7. Check portrait width at 320px, 375px and 430px, rotating to landscape if possible.

Note: Instamart is a manual shopping handoff, not in-app checkout. Recipe recommendations use deterministic demo rules, not live Gemini.
